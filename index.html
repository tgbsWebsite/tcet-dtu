FAQ'S

Frequently Asked
Questions

Have questions about our school, classes, or how things work? Look here first for quick help!

Q

What is the admission process at TCET University?

The admission process typically involves filling out an online application form, submitting required academic documents, and, depending on the programme, passing an entrance examination or personal interview.

Q

What are the eligibility criteria for admission to TCET University programmes?

Eligibility varies by programme. Most undergraduate courses require a recognized high school diploma (10+2) with minimum qualifying marks, while postgraduate courses require a relevant bachelor's degree.

Q

What is the fee structure for programmes at TCET University?

The fee structure is programme-specific and is updated annually. You can find detailed, transparent fee breakdowns on our official website or in the admissions brochure provided upon request.

Q

Does TCET University offer scholarships or financial assistance to students?

Yes, TCET University offers a range of merit-based scholarships and need-based financial aid. Eligible students can apply for these during the admission cycle.

Q

Does TCET University provide placement and career support?

Absolutely. We have a dedicated placement cell that actively partners with top industry leaders. We provide comprehensive career counseling, mock interviews, and organize campus recruitment drives.

Q

Can students from outside Mumbai apply to TCET University?

Yes! We welcome students from all across India and the globe. We also offer secure and well-equipped hostel facilities for outstation students.

Q

Does TCET University offer programmes or pathways suitable for working professionals?

Yes, we offer several executive programmes, part-time degrees, and flexible learning pathways designed specifically to accommodate the schedules of working professionals.

Q

Where is TCET University located?

TCET University is proudly located in Mumbai, Maharashtra. Our campus is equipped with state-of-the-art facilities and is easily accessible via public transportation.
